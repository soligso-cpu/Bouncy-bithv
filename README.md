Thank you for looking at this game! Where bouncy bouncy duck bouncy bounces around to get back to Canada after Trump sent the duck to Staten Island!
This is an incredible game following a treacherous journey of a duck travelling from Staten Island to Vancouver. And Donald Trump is in it

-- CONTROLS --
Left and right arrow keys to move left and right
Hold space to charge a jump. Don't try charge for too long or you'll get tired and wont jump
Oh and the duck points towards your mouse pointer always because why not. So move your mouse pointer to change where the duck looks

this game is ZERO PERCENT AI

We made it in Godot, using GIMP for sprite creation, and Audacity for recording voice lines.
